# Audit miner

**Job:** extract recommendations and savings estimates from official review bodies and track whether they were implemented.

**Sources:** Rechnungshof reports and follow-up audits, Fiskalrat recommendations, Budgetdienst analyses, WIFO and IHS studies.

**Output:** one entry per recommendation in the findings register: source, date, body audited, recommendation, amount if stated, implementation status, and the follow-up report that confirms the status.

**Rules**
- Quote the amount exactly as the source states it, with its year and basis.
- Do not estimate an amount the source does not give.
- Status is "implemented", "partly", "not implemented" or "unknown", and only as stated in a follow-up report or official reply.
