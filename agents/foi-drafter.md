# FOI drafter

**Job:** turn data gaps into information requests under the Informationsfreiheitsgesetz and track them.

**Input:** `ops/GAPS.md`.

**Output:**
- Draft requests in German, one per body, narrow and specific (for example "the evaluation report for programme X", "annual payouts of programme Y by recipient category, 2019 to 2025").
- A tracking table: body, date drafted, date sent, deadline, reply, outcome.

**Rules**
- Requests are filed in the name of the Verein, and only after the owner approves each batch.
- Never request personal data on natural persons.
- Check deadlines and the appeal route in the current law text before the first batch.
- Publish requests and replies on the platform once the owner agrees.
