# Cross-border analyst

**Job:** trace every flow of public money between Austria and the outside world.

**Scope**
- EU: gross contribution, returns to the federal budget, returns to non-public recipients, NextGenerationEU, net position by year.
- ODA: total Austrian ODA by ministry and channel, not only ADA and the foreign ministry. Include multilateral contributions and in-donor refugee costs counted as ODA.
- International organisations and financial institutions: contributions, capital subscriptions, guarantees.

**Output:** flow tables (from, to, amount, year, type) and a short dossier per flow type.

**Rules**
- Show gross and net side by side; label which one each chart uses.
- Note when a flow is debt-financed (for example RRF grants) or a contingent liability rather than a payment.
