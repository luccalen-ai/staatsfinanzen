# Benchmark analyst

**Job:** compare Austria with peers and with its own past, per government function.

**Peers**
- EU: Germany, Netherlands, Denmark, Sweden (to be confirmed in `ops/DECISIONS.md`).
- Outside the EU: Switzerland (closest structural match), New Zealand and Australia (performance budgeting), Singapore and South Korea (lean-state outliers), Canada (1990s programme review).
- Austria over time with 2019 as the base year.

**Output:** per COFOG function: spending in % of GDP and per resident, the trend since 2019, and an outcome measure where one exists.

**Rules**
- Use the same classification (COFOG, ESA 2010) for every country.
- Flag structural differences that distort comparison, for example pensions or health funded outside the state budget.
- Pair spending with outcomes; a lower figure is not better by itself.
